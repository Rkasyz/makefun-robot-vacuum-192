A one-line entry for the make.fun competition "The Robot Vacuum's
Funniest Excuse": the excuse a robot vacuum gives for missing a spot.

## The entry (120 characters)

> I missed that spot because the dust bunny filed a restraining order, and after what I saw under the couch, I respect it.

120 characters, one line, inside the gate of 1–120 characters. The
excuse lands in two beats: first the absurd legal twist — a dust bunny
filed a restraining order — then the vacuum backing down after what it
saw under the couch. A machine making peace with failure, in its own
deadpan voice, with the humour drawn from a vacuum's world: dust,
bunnies, and the territory under the couch.

## How to run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

This writes `entry.txt` into the output directory, containing exactly
the line above, with no trailing newline.
